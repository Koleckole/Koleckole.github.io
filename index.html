About me

I'm a Magna Cum Laude graduate from the University of Jamestown with a BA in Communications, a concentration in Sports Communication, and a minor in Web Design. I'm driven by a genuine passion for building things — whether that's a data pipeline that surfaces a business insight, or a website built from scratch that actually gets used by real people.

My background spans data analytics, front-end web development, and operational coordination across industrial project environments. I've worked in the field alongside project managers at PME Industrial and EllisDon, where I learned how data-driven decisions get made in fast-moving, high-stakes environments — and how to communicate those decisions clearly across teams.

Web development is something I pursue beyond the classroom. I built innovationhomeshow.ca — a full commercial trade show platform with ticketing, exhibitor directories, a live schedule, and integrated payments — entirely from scratch using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I love the challenge of taking an idea and making it real, clean, and functional.

I'm especially interested in opportunities within the energy industry, where I can combine my analytical skills and communication background to support data-driven operations, logistics, and performance tracking.
